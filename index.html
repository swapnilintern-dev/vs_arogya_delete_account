VS Arogya — account and data deletion

This page explains how to delete your VS Arogya account and its associated personal data. VS Arogya is published on Google Play by VS Arogya.

Option 1 — Delete from the app (instant)

  1. Open the VS Arogya app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Privacy & Security.
  4. Tap Delete Account, then confirm with Delete Permanently.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, send us an email from your registered email address. Please include:

  • Your registered email address and mobile number
  • Your store / business name
Email deletion request

Or write to vsarogyameda@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request". You can also call or WhatsApp +91 90656 11226. To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm the request. We will delete the account within 7 working days and confirm by email.

What happens to your data

Deleted
  • Profile: store name, contact person, mobile number, email
  • Business address and saved delivery addresses
  • GST and drug licence details
  • Login password and active sessions
  • Cart, saved products and notification preferences
Kept (required by law)
  • Orders, invoices and payment records are kept for as long as Indian GST, accounting and drug-sale laws require. They are used only for those legal purposes.

Email OTPs used for sign-in are deleted automatically after they expire. For more details, see our Privacy Policy, or contact vsarogyameda@gmail.com.
